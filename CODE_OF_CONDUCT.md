# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to making participation in this project harassment-free and respectful
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and precise.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and claims, never people.
- Assume good faith; ask for clarification before assigning intent.
- Welcome correction. A reproducible counterexample wins over seniority.
- Keep public discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Bad-faith reports, deliberate disruption, or persistent off-topic discussion.

## Enforcement

Report violations through GitHub's private security advisory channel or a minimal
public issue requesting a private contact. Do not repost sensitive material.

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently ban participants for behavior inconsistent with this code.
